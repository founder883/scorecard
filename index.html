Frequently Asked

Answers before you ask.

Still have questions? Talk to a solar expert — free consultation, no pressure.

How much does a rooftop solar system cost in India?

A quality 3 kW residential system costs between ₹1.8–2.4 lakh before subsidy. After central and state subsidies, effective cost is typically ₹1.2–1.6 lakh. EMI plans start at ₹2,500/month.

How much roof space do I need?

Around 100 sq ft of shadow-free rooftop per 1 kW of solar. A 3 kW system needs roughly 300 sq ft. Our engineer will confirm this during the free site survey.

What happens on cloudy days or at night?

Grid-tied systems automatically draw from the grid when solar output is low, and export excess to the grid on sunny days through net metering — you're billed only on the net units.

Do you handle the subsidy paperwork?

Yes. We take care of the entire process on the National Portal for Rooftop Solar. The subsidy is transferred directly to your bank account after commissioning.

What is the warranty?

Solar panels: 25-year performance warranty, 12-year product warranty. Inverter: 5–10 years. Batteries (if opted): 10 years. Full workmanship warranty from SunSquare for 5 years.

How long does installation take?

Physical installation is typically completed in 3–5 days. Full commissioning (including DISCOM approval and net-meter installation) takes 3–6 weeks depending on your state.
